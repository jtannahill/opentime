Quickly see when you're free. Open Time reads your device calendars and shows your open time slots. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.

FEATURES
- View free and busy slots for today, tomorrow, or any date range
- Choose which calendars count: exclude holidays, sports schedules, or shared calendars, and every list, widget, and share reflects only the calendars you pick
- Slot duration picker (15, 30, or 60 minutes). Chips subdivide your free blocks at the chosen interval
- Long-press any chip for Open in Calendar, Create Event Here, or Copy Time
- Long-press any day name to Share or Copy that day's availability to anywhere (Messages, Mail, Notes)
- Light, dark, or system appearance with an in-app picker
- Pull to refresh, and availability refreshes automatically after you create or edit an event
- Show or hide busy events
- Toggle weekends on or off
- This Week, Next Week, and Next 14 Days quick picks
- Custom date range picker
- Home screen widgets (small and medium). Tap any chip to jump straight to that slot in the app
- Live Activities and Dynamic Island for real-time free/busy on your lock screen
- Apple Intelligence query bar on supported devices: type "tomorrow afternoon for 30 min" or "any hour Friday afternoon" and get the matching slots
- Siri and Shortcuts: try "What's my availability in Open Time" or "When am I free in Open Time"
- Apple Watch companion to check your week from your wrist
- Share Extension to drop your availability into any app from the share sheet
- iMessage app to pick days and send your availability without leaving the conversation

PRIVACY FIRST
Your calendar data stays on your device. No accounts. No analytics. No third-party servers. Zero data collection.
Open Time uses Apple's EventKit to read your calendars locally. Nothing ever leaves your device.
Perfect for freelancers, consultants, and anyone who needs to quickly share when they're available.
