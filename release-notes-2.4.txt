Fewer taps, start to finish.

SHARE sends your pick-a-time link straight away. Press and hold it for link settings or to send your times as text.

Add your number or email to the link, and their Send button opens Messages or Mail already addressed to you. It is optional, and anyone with the link can see it.

Confirm is now one tap. The meeting goes on your calendar and the share sheet opens with their confirmation. Edit and invite is still there when you want to add them as an invitee.
