Fewer taps, start to finish.

SHARE sends your pick-a-time link straight away. Press and hold it for link settings or to send your times as text.

Add your number or email to the link, and their Send button opens Messages or Mail already addressed to you. It is optional, and anyone with the link can see it.

Confirm is one tap. When someone sends their picks back, the meeting goes on your calendar and the share sheet opens with their confirmation. They get a link that shows the meeting in their own time zone and adds it to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook in one tap.

Want them as an invitee instead? Edit and invite opens the new-event sheet with the meeting filled in, and your calendar sends the invitation.
