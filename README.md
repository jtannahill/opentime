> iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch app that shows your calendar availability at a glance. Reads your calendars on-device; no accounts, no servers, no tracking. Send your free time as a pick-a-time link: the other person chooses up to five times and sends them back, and you confirm one.

**App Store:** [OpenTime](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/opentime/id6760204246), $0.99 one time (no in-app purchases, no subscription). Current version 2.2.

**Website:** [lulls.app](https://lulls.app)

## Features

- **Calendar Availability**: Visual free/busy time slots for today, tomorrow, or any custom range; weekends optional
- **Choose Which Calendars Count**: Leave out holidays, sports schedules, or shared calendars; the app, widgets, Live Activities, iMessage, Siri, and the share sheet all respect the pick (Apple Watch uses all calendars for now)
- **Natural-Language Queries**: "tomorrow afternoon for 30 min" via on-device Apple Intelligence on supported devices
- **Duration Chips**: 15, 30, or 60 minute slots; long-press to open in Calendar, create an event, or copy
- **Interactive Widgets**: Small and medium home screen widgets that surface the next free slot
- **Live Activities & Dynamic Island**: Real-time free/busy status on the lock screen
- **Pick-a-Time Links**: Send your free time as a link; it opens as an App Clip on iPhone (nothing to install) or in any browser, in the other person's time zone
- **Pick-a-Time Replies** (new in 2.2): They choose up to five times and send them back as a reply link; open it in OpenTime to see their picks in your time zone, confirm one, and add it to your calendar
- **iMessage Extension**: Pick days and send availability directly in Messages
- **Siri & Shortcuts**: voice commands such as "What's my availability in OpenTime", "When am I free in OpenTime", and "Share my availability from OpenTime"
- **Apple Watch**: Standalone watchOS companion showing next 5 weekdays
- **Share Extension**: Copy availability from any app's share sheet

## Stack

- Swift / SwiftUI
- WidgetKit (interactive widgets, Live Activities)
- ActivityKit (Dynamic Island)
- EventKit
- AppIntents (Siri, Shortcuts)
- App Clip (`OpenTimeClip`, opens pick-a-time links with nothing to install)
- FoundationModels (on-device natural-language queries)
- WatchKit / watchOS
- Cloudflare Pages (lulls.app, the browser pick and reply pages, and the MCP server as a Pages Function)

## Fonts

The app's display face is Neue Haas Grotesk Display, a commercial typeface licensed separately, so the font files are not in the public repository ([github.com/jtannahill/opentime](https://github.com/jtannahill/opentime)). To build with it, add your own licensed `NHGDisplay-Regular.ttf`, `NHGDisplay-Medium.ttf` and `NHGDisplay-Bold.ttf` to `OpenTimeUI/Sources/OpenTimeUI/Resources/`. Without them the app builds and runs with the system font.

## Pick-a-time links and the MCP server

Pick-a-time links (`https://lulls.app/pick#...`) carry free times in the URL fragment, encoded by `OpenTimeUI/Sources/OpenTimeUI/Share/PickLink.swift`. They open the `OpenTimeClip` App Clip, the app when installed, or `docs/pick.html` in any browser.

The recipient can choose up to five times and send them back as a reply link (`https://lulls.app/reply#...`, `PickReply` in the same file), which carries only those times, the meeting length, an optional name, and their time zone. Replies open in the app (`PickReplyView`) or `docs/reply.html` in a browser. Universal links for `/pick*` and `/reply*`, and the App Clip association, live in `docs/.well-known/apple-app-site-association`.

`https://lulls.app/mcp` is a public, stateless MCP server (Cloudflare Pages Function `functions/mcp.js`, logic in `mcp/`) with two tools, `create_pick_link` and `read_pick_link` (which also reads reply links). `mcp/pick.js` mirrors the Swift wire format; `node --test mcp/test/pick.test.js` and the Swift `PickLinkTests` check both directions.

Add it to Claude: Settings > Connectors > Add custom connector, URL `https://lulls.app/mcp`.

## Simulator screenshots and recordings

Debug builds take launch arguments so the App Store screenshots and the lulls.app clip can be made without a real calendar or a real link tap:

- `-seedDemoCalendar` fills the default calendar with a believable week (seeded events are marked and replaced on every run)
- `-openURL <url>` routes a `lulls.app/pick#...` or `lulls.app/reply#...` link as if it had been opened
- `-pickSelect <n>` preselects `n` times on the pick screen
- `-pickLink <url>` opens a link in the App Clip

With no Simulator window, a UI test can drive the installed app through `XCUIApplication(bundleIdentifier:)` with these arguments while `xcrun simctl io <device> recordVideo` records the screen.
