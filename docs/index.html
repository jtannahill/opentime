Questions people ask.

Does it work with Google Calendar or Outlook?
Yes, if those accounts are set up in the iPhone's Calendar app. OpenTime reads them through Apple's EventKit and never talks to Google or Microsoft itself.
Do I need Apple Intelligence?
Only for typing questions in plain words. Everything else works on any supported iPhone.
Does it book meetings?
No. A pick-a-time link lets them choose up to five times and send them back; you confirm one and add it to your calendar. OpenTime does not hold the time for you, and there is no account or booking page to manage.
What does it cost?
$0.99, once. No subscription and no in-app purchases.
What do I need?
iOS 26.2 or later on iPhone, iPadOS 26.2 or later on iPad, and watchOS 10 or later on Apple Watch.
